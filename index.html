HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP
Important boundary. V0.3 can learn examples of sounds, visible movements and some visual scenes or objects. Image embeddings do not guarantee recognition of arbitrary objects or actions. The app does not interpret meaning independently or reliably identify every sound or movement. It needs supervised examples, verification with the person, and real-world accuracy testing. Not suitable as the only means of urgent or medical communication.

Signals for this person

Train what *isn't* a message, too.

Add examples such as coughing, scratching, background conversation, fidgeting or random hand movement as Ignore signals. To make any match possible, KIN currently requires at least three examples of two different signals in the same category (or one message plus an Ignore signal). Aim for considerably more examples across days, environments and positions.
