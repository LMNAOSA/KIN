HOW IT WORKS

Teach it your way.

1. Create a message. Choose a phrase or label any natural signal as a message.

2. Teach the signal. Record several examples from the same person in varied conditions. For movement, use the positioning guide; for sound, record natural vocalisations.

3. Test communication. Watch live mapping and review a possible meaning before confirming it aloud. Ambiguous signals are left unrecognised.

Profiles remain in your browser. Export them to keep backups. The prototype needs internet access to load face/hand models and has not been clinically validated.
